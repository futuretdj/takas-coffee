Ten minutes and twenty seconds

Every batch is logged second by second. Scroll, and the curve below is drawn the way Tomas watched it on Thursday at 07:12.

  1. ChargeThe drum is at 200 °C. 12 kg of green coffee goes in and the temperature falls to 92 °C in eighty seconds.
  2. DryingUntil about 150 °C the beans lose their water. They turn from green to straw yellow and smell like hay and fresh bread.
  3. BrowningSugars and amino acids react. This is where the sweetness is built, so we slow the gas down and give it three minutes.
  4. First crackAt 8:40 the beans pop like popcorn. For a filter coffee we let it develop for only 100 seconds more.
  5. DropAt 208 °C the beans fall into the cooling tray and are cold within four minutes. Then they rest for five days.

From the farm to your door in nine days

We buy from four farms and one washing station, by name, and pay above the fair-trade price for every kilo.

  1. The green coffee

    Each lot arrives in 60 kg jute bags from Nyeri, Guji and Huila. We cup three samples before we buy, and reject about half of what we are sent.

  2. Thursday, 07:00

    Six batches on a 15 kg Giesen. The trier comes out every thirty seconds after yellowing, so we can smell and see the beans, not only read the numbers.

  3. Cooled, rested, packed

    The beans rest for a day, then go into bags with a one-way valve. Each bag is weighed by hand and stamped with the batch and the roast date.

  4. At your door by Tuesday

    Orders before Thursday at noon leave on Friday with DPD. Vilnius is next day, Riga and Warsaw two days, Berlin and Paris three.

    Questions

    Anything else: hello@takas.example

    How fresh is the coffee when it arrives?

    We roast on Thursday and post on Friday, so it reaches most of the EU two to five days after roasting. Filter coffee tastes best from day 5 to day 30, espresso from day 8 to day 40. The roast date is on every bag.

    Should I buy whole beans or ground?

    Whole beans keep their taste about three times longer. If you do not have a grinder, choose the grind for your brewer and we grind it on the morning it ships, on a Mahlkönig EK43. Ground coffee is best within two weeks.

    Where do you ship, and how much does it cost?

    Lithuania €3.90, free from €40. Latvia, Estonia and Poland €5.90, the rest of the EU €7.90, free from €60. We ship with DPD and you get a tracking link on Friday.

    What if I do not like a coffee?

    Write to us within 30 days and we send a different one, or refund you. We would rather you drink something you like, and it helps us know what to roast.

    Are the bags recyclable?

    The bags are paper with a thin plant-based lining and go in the paper bin once the valve is removed. In the café you can refill your own tin and save €1.

    Can I visit the roastery?

    Yes. On the first Saturday of the month we run a cupping at 11:00 for twelve people, €15 with a 100 g bag to take home. Ask at the bar or write to us.
